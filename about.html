About

Hi, I'm Matt.

I'm a software engineer and home-recording musician in the Philadelphia area.

Day job

Backend, cloud, and AI agents

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at JPMorgan Chase, where I'm a technical lead on a high-volume home-loan platform. Most of my work is Java/Spring Boot microservices and Python services on AWS, with the infrastructure written in Terraform. Before that I built e-commerce systems at Qurate Retail Group and ERP software at Deacom.

I use agentic AI development every day: writing specs, handing pieces to coding agents, and reviewing what comes back like any other pull request. This site was rebuilt that way.

AWS Certified Developer – Associate. B.S. in Computer Science from West Chester University.

Off the clock

Music

I play classical guitar, bass and synthesizers, and record originals and covers at home across a bunch of genres. Improvising music and building software feel similar to me: you start without the whole thing planned out, and the good parts come from the back-and-forth.
