The Basement

My basement theater runs on Home Assistant and a Raspberry Pi. Here are three problems it had to solve, played out on a copy of the real rules. Nothing here talks to my actual house.

Playing · tap any control to take over
Rule Empty basement Nobody's downstairs, so every light is off. See the code

01

Coming or going?

Motion sensors see movement, not people. With a door sensor at the top of the stairs and one motion sensor at the bottom, the order they fire says who's coming and who's going: a version of the "wasp in a box" problem.

The case it can't see is someone sitting still. I'm adding mmWave presence sensors to fix that.

02

Don't flicker the lights

Pausing a movie waits 4 seconds before the lights come up, so flipping through a menu doesn't strobe the room.

Touchdown flashes change brightness, never color. Fast color changes flood the bulbs' Zigbee network.

03

When in doubt, leave it on

The projector's lamp needs its fans after shutdown. Power gets cut only after a 90-second cooldown and two near-zero current readings in a row.

No reading, no cut: a plug left on costs cents, a cut mid-cooldown costs a lamp.
