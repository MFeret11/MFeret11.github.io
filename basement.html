Theater Automation

I wanted a place to relax without juggling three remotes or getting up to turn off the lights. My solution: Home Assistant on a Raspberry Pi. Here's how it grew, one problem and one fix at a time.

Why I started

No getting up for the lights

We'd get comfortable for a movie, and then someone had to get up and walk to the light switch by the stairs. That's where this started.

More

Three devices meant three remotes, and the projector takes a while to warm up, so it's nice to start it before I even head downstairs. Mostly, I wanted a great place to unwind.

Chapter 1 of 6

Coming and going

The first version turned the lights on when the stair door opened. But a door can't tell coming from going.

Try it

Nobody's there, and the lights are still on.

Door, then motion: coming down. Motion, then door: leaving, so the lights go off.

More

The sensors are a Third Reality Zigbee door sensor and motion sensor. "Leaving" means the door opens within 25 seconds of motion at the bottom. It was 18 seconds until the sensor history showed a slow climb that took 18.76. The full story · See the code

Chapter 2 of 6

More than one person

Then two people came down. When one left, the room decided everyone had.

Try it

The lights went out on the person still on the couch. Motion sensors can't see someone sitting still.

The mmWave sensor can, so the lights stay on.

More

The sensor is a Sonoff SNZB-06P, also Zigbee. Once the room has been quiet for 30 minutes, the lights go off. If the sensor goes offline, the door and motion rules take over again. See the code

During the show

Movie on, lights down. Pause to grab a snack, and a dim glow comes up, with only the stairs lit on the way.

More

The glow comes from a Leviton Matter dimmer at 3%. In the real room it waits 4 seconds after a pause, so tapping through a menu doesn't flicker the lights. The stairs fade to 2% candlelight for a minute. See the code

Chapter 5 of 6

Music without the projector

Music doesn't need the projector. But cutting its power while the lamp is hot can ruin it.

Try it

Projector current0.00 A

The lamp is warm, so the projector stays on.

Its power stays on. Standby draws about 0.01 A, and the remote's power button can wake it again.

Putting it back to sleep is safe. Cutting its power isn't, so the guard never does.

More

The projector plugs into a Third Reality Zigbee smart plug that measures current, so Home Assistant can tell "lamp on" from "fans only." When a session ends, the projector goes to standby over HDMI-CEC and its own fans cool the lamp, with the power left on. Music cuts the power only when the projector is already cold, so HDMI-CEC can't light the lamp for a song. If the lamp stays lit for 2 minutes while nobody's using the theater, a guard puts it back to sleep and sends a notification, without ever cutting the power. The full story · See the code

Chapter 6 of 6

Game day

Live sports come from an antenna in the attic and an HDHomeRun tuner, not a subscription. With Tailscale, family can watch from anywhere, even my dad on a sailboat mid-ocean.

Try it

Brightness only, never color: fast color changes flood the Zigbee network.

More

The HDHomeRun turns the antenna signal into a stream on my home network. Nothing in the house is open to the internet: family devices reach it through Tailscale, a private network only invited devices can join. See the code

Summary

What I learned

Mostly, networking: how devices talk to each other.

Zigbee
Bulbs, sensors and smart plugs through a Sonoff USB dongle, in a mesh that gets stronger as it grows.
Matter
The Leviton theater dimmer.
MoCA
Connects the basement to my router over the coax already in the walls: fast and stable, with no new cables.
Home network
The Yamaha receiver, Onn streaming box and HDHomeRun tuner.
MQTT
Status from Batocera and the gaming PC.
Wake-on-LAN
Turns the gaming PC on.
Tailscale
Family watching from anywhere.

I also found the open-source community, and shared my own config. And I got what I set out to build: a room where I can sit down, press one button, and relax.
