    What's real here

    The rules are the real ones from my Home Assistant config, simplified. A playing movie or game blacks out the room and leaves only the bias lights behind the screen. Pausing a movie waits 4 seconds before bringing up a 1% glow, so a menu blip doesn't flicker the lights. Music stays fully dark on purpose. An Eagles game flips the room into Social mode and paints it Kelly green.

    The receiver's sound program follows what's on: Standard for movies, Straight for retro games, 7ch Stereo for music and Sports for football. The touchdown flash pulses brightness between 100% and 15% without ever changing color, because changing colors that fast floods the Zigbee mesh.

    Ending the session runs the same ordered shutdown as the real one. The part I care most about is the projector: its lamp needs the fans after power-off, so the relay opens only after a 90-second cooldown and two consecutive current readings at or below 0.06 A. Unplug the sensor and the system refuses to cut power. The story behind that rule.
